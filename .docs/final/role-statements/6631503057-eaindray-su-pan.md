# Personal Role Statement — AI Lead

**Eaindray Su Pan — 6631503057**

As AI Lead, I connect the legal rules our product must follow to the AI
agents that actually enforce them. I wrote rule.md, turning three Thai laws
— PDPA, the Computer Crime Act, and the Electronic Transactions Act — into
clear rules: ask consent before collecting sensitive dietary data, disclose
and limit what we send to third-party AI, keep access logs for 90 days, and
save every AI-generated recipe so it can be reproduced later if a user
disputes it. I didn't stop at writing rules on paper — I built them into how
the team actually works, by updating requirement-writer.md so every
requirement spec includes legal requirements automatically instead of
relying on someone remembering to add them. I also wrote backlog-auditor.md
and design-writer.md so those same rules stay traceable through the backlog
and into the design diagrams, instead of getting lost after our product
pivot. On top of that, I wrote three skills (capture-requirement,
audit-backlog, audit-design) so the rest of the team can keep following
this process on their own, without me checking every file by hand. We're
still in the DISCOVER phase, so I haven't written product code yet — but the
compliance foundation I built is what every later feature, especially the
AI recipe generator, will have to follow.
