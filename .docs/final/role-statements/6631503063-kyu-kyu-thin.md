# Personal Role Statement — QA/Test

**Kyu Kyu Thin — 6631503063**

As a QA/Test member of Team Apex, my main responsibility was to ensure that
What2Cook worked correctly and provided a good user experience. I focused
on testing the application, identifying defects, and verifying that the
system met its requirements.

I prepared the Test Plan, which included 15 test cases and 5 User
Acceptance Testing (UAT) tasks, each with clear and measurable pass or fail
criteria. I also developed two functional requirements: F6 (Dietary
Filters) and F7 (Search by Ingredient). In addition, I conducted UAT
sessions with real users, including U07, U08, U09, and U10. I recorded task
completion times, errors, and their severity to evaluate the application's
performance and usability. I also used the System Usability Scale (SUS)
questionnaire, where the application achieved a score of 71.3, above the
average benchmark of 68. Through testing, I identified 17 defects, including
slow AI recipe generation, incorrect leftover quantities, and receipt
scanning failures. I worked with the team to ensure these issues were fixed
and verified through the second round of testing.

This experience improved my attention to detail, problem-solving, and
teamwork skills. Most importantly, I learned that effective quality
assurance requires clear criteria, reliable evidence, and continuous
improvement.
