# Work log — 2026-09-06 (W4)

## Admin / moderator role removed from the product

The team decided the product has no admin or moderator actor this phase. The
only human actors are the Home Cook and (Could) a Household Member. Everything
that assumed an admin was removed; the parts of LR9 that do not depend on an
admin were kept.

## What was deliberately kept

- **LR6 (CCA §26)** still says a recipe removed from view after a complaint or
  safety issue is retained internally with its log. That is a retention duty on
  the system, not an admin feature — the takedown is a team/operational action
  outside the product UI. Nothing in it names an admin.
- **LR9** keeps the "no certified / nutritionist-approved language" rule. It is
  ETA §28 and has nothing to do with an admin role.
- `20260902-log.md` was left unedited — it is a historical record of that day.

Ids were **not** renumbered: LR9, B17, and UC1–UC12 keep their existing numbers
so earlier traces stay valid.
